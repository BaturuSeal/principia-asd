# Gas transport through a porous membrane

Separate molecule-wall and viscous transport contributions to nitrogen flux through a hydrotalcite-coated support. Entire temperature regimes are held out, with calibrated absolute pressures and source flux units.

## Scientific contract

Single S-HT membrane and nitrogen only. Whole nominal temperatures: train 25/200/500 Celsius; dev 350; test 300/400/450. Actual temperatures remain inputs. Source validation sheet and other gases/materials are outside this contract.

- Single S-HT membrane, pure nitrogen; no independent membrane replication
- Known transport families, not direct pore-size identification
- Additive effective Knudsen and viscous contributions
- A viscosity power law approximates the finite measured temperature range
