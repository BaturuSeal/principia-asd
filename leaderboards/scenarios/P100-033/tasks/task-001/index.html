P100-033 · PPG source orientation and processing require review

All 3,888 recordings stay grouped by 50 participants, including 3,058 quality-0 records. Forty-eight source headers encode 300 scalar channels in one frame; their historical temporal interpretation is preserved and flagged. ECG references and human quality labels never enter predictors.

This estimates simultaneous source ECG-reference heart rate after a full PPG segment. It does not establish clinical performance, future prediction or new-device transfer. Rule complexity excludes 3,888 segment detrend fits, scale estimates and spectral/autocorrelation processing. Source orientation and annotation provenance require review.

Within-task accuracy gate then accuracy/expanded-complexity Pareto; sensitivity diagnostic only. Scientific review: not_assessed.
