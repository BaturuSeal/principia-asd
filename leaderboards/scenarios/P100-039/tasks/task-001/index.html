P100-039 · Sample earnings classification, not population or causal inference

All 5,290 eligible respondents preserve whole country/hash-block splits. Earnings are country-relative deciles, education is source-derived and ages are fixed bin midpoints. Positive survey weights affect eligibility but are absent from compact scoring; 3,640 source records are excluded.

Current scores are unweighted within-block Brier losses with equal block aggregation; they do not reproduce historical survey-weighted losses or establish population estimates. Survey clusters, replicate-weight variance, prospective earnings, country transfer and causal returns are not established. All existing metrics and outcomes remain unchanged.

Within-task accuracy gate then accuracy/expanded-complexity Pareto; sensitivity diagnostic only. Scientific review: not_assessed.
