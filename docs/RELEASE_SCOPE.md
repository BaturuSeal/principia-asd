# Release scope

Software 1.0.0rc4 introduces editable per-workspace SiliconFlow model profiles, a portable public-release layout and a prefix-aware static deployment. Its scientific data and leaderboard editions remain 1.0.0rc2 with exact hashes in `configs/edition-compatibility.json`. No campaign was rerun and no score was changed for this deployment.

The primary record is 40 scenarios × 13 configurations = 520 attempts, with 483 completed and 37 failed. It contains 693 rules, 684 valid assessments and nine invalid outputs. Engineering cohorts and their failures remain separate. Counts do not imply scientific success.

Known preparation defects remain in P100-012, 019, 062, 070, 086 and 089. Source-specific review remains unfinished for P100-002, 035 and 045. At least 65 historical rules have incomplete learned-state complexity profiles; original numbers are preserved, with advisories. Scientific review, novelty, causality and practical impact remain unassessed. Source rights and scientific admission are not certified by this software handoff or by the owner's deployment request.

The public library includes prepared numeric examples and existing results. Local custom imports currently accept grouped numeric CSV/CSV.gz (10 MB expanded); arbitrary multimedia decoding is not a generally admitted user workflow. Custom input/output tasks receive separate identities. Unavailable methods remain disabled, and frozen development protocols explicitly restrict model configurations and platforms. Normal methods accept validated custom SiliconFlow profiles.

The companion has no private targets for the shared examples. New runs can fit, select and freeze rules locally; final private-test loss stays pending unless an authorized evaluator supplies a protected assessment. Each user's own imported targets and results stay on their computer. The server's administrative evaluator is not an upload service.

Detailed historical source reviews, conformance checks and research records remain under `docs/` and `leaderboards/receipts/`. Older dated receipts describe their historical software versions; the rc4 delivery and deployment receipts identify the current files. `WORK_STATUS.md` and previous acceptance receipts are retained in the development checkout, not promoted as current public product documentation.
